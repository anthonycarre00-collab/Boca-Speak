# BocaSpeak — brand direction

## Working name
**BocaSpeak**

The name deliberately combines the Spanish word **boca** (mouth) with the English idea of speaking. It points directly at the product's original insight: English becomes easier when learners can get the sound into their mouths.

## Working tagline
**English you can actually say.**

Short, human, and deliberately not "AI"-sounding. It also describes the product's promise without claiming native-level speech or accent elimination.

## Visual direction
The prototype uses a warm editorial palette rather than the usual blue/purple AI aesthetic:
- deep ink green
- warm paper/cream
- coral accent
- muted sage
- small brass/gold highlights

Typography is a friendly sans for interface text plus a soft editorial serif for learning moments and large headlines.

The mouth mark is intentionally abstract: a small, slightly playful speech/mouth shape rather than a microphone, headset, flag or generic language icon.

## Product personality
Warm, clever, encouraging, grown-up, slightly playful.

Not childish. Not corporate. Not "AI tutor" branding.

## Naming note
BocaSpeak is a **working product name**, not a trademark/domain clearance. Before public launch, perform a proper trademark, domain, App Store/Google Play and social-handle check in Colombia and the markets where the product will operate.
