# Melissa

**Tagline:** Aprende en español. Habla en inglés.

Melissa is a Spanish-first English-learning product inspired by a real learner experience: the student first understands the idea in Spanish, then hears English, then uses a learner-friendly pronunciation bridge, and finally practises without the bridge.

The name is personal to the project and refers to the student who inspired the idea.

Visual language: warm cream, deep ink green, terracotta/coral, muted botanical green. The app icon uses a friendly illustrated young Colombian/Latina character with dark curls and speech marks, rather than a microphone, flag or generic lettermark.

The pronunciation bridge is intentionally approximate, not IPA. It must remain teacher-editable and must never be presented as the official pronunciation of English.
