# Melissa — prototype direction

## Core product
Spanish-first English practice for Spanish-speaking learners. The defining feature is the learner-friendly pronunciation bridge: Spanish is the starting point, English is the destination, and the bridge is scaffolding that should gradually disappear.

## Current prototype priorities
1. Clear first-run onboarding in Spanish.
2. Curated bridge content across high-value Spanish→English pronunciation problems.
3. Practical phrases and situations rather than isolated vocabulary only.
4. Listen → say → hide the bridge → say again.
5. Student feedback on whether each bridge is useful or confusing.
6. Offline-friendly local persistence and PWA installation.
7. Teacher Studio kept lightweight until real student usage shows what matters.

## Small quality-of-life features in v0.6
- self-selected pronunciation focus;
- sound-specific coaching cards;
- saved words/phrases;
- personal practice speed;
- simple difficult-item resurfacing;
- local recording playback;
- daily three-practice nudge;
- bilingual phrase tester using only reviewed bridge entries.

## Deliberately not included yet
- AI chat tutor;
- live pronunciation scoring;
- leaderboards/gamification systems;
- complex accounts or social features;
- cloud audio storage;
- large-scale teacher automation.

Those may be useful later, but they would make the current student test less focused.

## Content rule
Every bridge entry should be reviewed for: English target accuracy, Spanish readability, regional clarity for Colombian learners, mouth/action cue, usefulness in real speech, and whether the bridge helps the learner move toward the actual English rather than replacing it.
